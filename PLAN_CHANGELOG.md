# Plan Changelog & Ground-Truth Adaptations

This log records every discrepancy discovered against assumptions or required adaptation to match the `./DT-HSBP` codebase and Windows 10 environment.

## Phase 2.1 — Baseline Schema & Report Metrics Grounding (2026-11-14)
- **Stochastic & Evaluation Grounding**: `StochasticExperiment.m` and `ExperimentSmokeTest.m` sit at `./DT-HSBP` root rather than `experiments/`. `runCompleteEvaluationAndReport.m` uses six DT-HSBP sections (`Batch-Leave`, `Lifespan-Sorted Tail Clustering`, `Zero-Latency Precomputation`, `Overall System Synthesis @ N=3000`, `Accuracy Sensitivity eta in [0.5, 1.0]`, and `Batch Departure Scalability M`) and computes LKH cost at `48 B/msg` (`32+16`) and TGDH at `128 B/token` vs. `56 B/msg` for `FlatSBP`/`HSBP`/`DTHSBP`. Recorded verbatim in `traces/schemas/matlab_metrics_schema.json` and `baseline_matlab_results/matlab_baseline_metrics.json`.

## Phase 2.2 — Trace Exporter & Simulation Instrumentation Adaptations (2026-11-14)
- **Boundary Projection for Unbounded `UAV.move()`**: `UAV.move(cfg)` advances `position = position + velocity * timeStep` without reflective or periodic bounds so `Predictor.predict` and `Residual` match linearly. Rather than mutating `UAV.m` physics, `TraceExporter.projectPosition(pos, areaX, areaY)` applies continuous specular reflection into `[0, areaX] x [0, areaY]` strictly at JSON serialization time.
- **Explicit Triggering `clusterID` for `FlatSBP` & Departure Capture**: `FlatSBP.m` returns `RekeyResult` with `clusterID = []`, and `protocol.leave`/`failure` removes the departing node before metrics logging. `Simulation.m` therefore captures `triggerClusterID` prior to protocol execution and passes it explicitly into `TraceExporter.buildTraceRecord(...)` so bounded `nodePositions` scoping (`<= clusterSize + 1`) and `clusterMembershipAfter` remain well-defined across all protocols.
- **Paired DT Physical Instability Under `cfg.enableTraceExport`**: `Simulation.schedulePredictedDepartures()` normally returns early when `~isa(obj.protocol, "DTHSBP")`, leaving reactive protocols with `0` DT-driven instability leaves. When `cfg.enableTraceExport == true`, `schedulePredictedDepartures()` isolates hazard draws with a deterministic per-step RNG stream, schedules `DTPredictedDepartureEvent` identically across all five protocols, and calls `uav.dt.cancelPrediction()` for non-`DTHSBP` protocols so `DTHSBP` batches predicted leaves while reactive protocols handle each physical departure individually at the exact same timestamp. When `cfg.enableTraceExport` is absent or `false`, baseline behavior is bit-for-bit unchanged.

## Phase 2.3 — LKH/TGDH DES Adapter Adaptation (2026-11-14)
- **Tree Protocols Outside Stochastic DES Harness**: `runStochasticJoinLeaveStudy.m` only evaluates `FlatSBP`, `HSBP`, and `DTHSBP`; `LKH(swarm, 3)` and `TGDH(swarm, 2)` are instantiated statically inside Section 4 of `runCompleteEvaluationAndReport.m` and draw internal `randi` keys during `join`/`leave`/`failure`. `TreeProtocolTraceAdapter` wraps `LKH`/`TGDH` inside `Simulation` while saving/restoring RNG state around initialization and operations (advancing RNG strictly by one scratch `UAV` construction on `join`), keeping exogenous Poisson event schedules and spatial trajectories synchronized across all 5 protocols.
- **Scale Trace Pretty-Printing**: `runHybridTraceExport.m` pretty-prints traces up to `N <= 250` (including committed `traces/samples/events_trace_<proto>_N50.json` fixtures) and uses compact JSON serialization for `N > 250` (`traces/runs/`, git-ignored) so `FlatSBP` at `N = 1000` remains fast and compact.
